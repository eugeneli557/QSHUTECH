What this is

A working prototype of a paid grant-discovery service for US small businesses, founders, and nonprofits. The eligibility engine, ranking, deadline logic, and paywall are all live in this page. Change your profile on the left and the results re-rank instantly — that instant "here are your 3 grants out of 500" is the thing people pay for and a Google search can't do.

The Total available to you figure sums the award ceilings of grants you're eligible for — the number that makes a $19/month subscription feel free.

Where the data comes from

These records are modeled on real US programs (USDA REAP, DOE SBIR, EPA EJ, CalSEED, NYSERDA, HUD CDBG…) in the exact schema the live pipeline produces. Federal data is free and key-less via grants.gov and usaspending.gov; state/local is added by targeted scraping + AI extraction.

Run the real pipeline: python fetch_federal.py --keyword solar --enrich. Every grant keeps its official source link — GrantRadar is the map, the government site is the destination.
